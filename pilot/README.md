# BusinessRun Public Trace Review — pilot

An agent says “done.” The screen says otherwise. We turn one small public browser-agent trace into a concrete evidence map and a retest plan.

**Pilot price: 29 USDC on Base, after delivery. No subscription or upfront payment.** This is a new pilot with no verified customer sales or validated conversion rate.

[Request a public trace review](https://github.com/Daisuke134/businessrun-bench/issues/new?template=public-trace-review.yml)

## What to submit

A public link or pasted, redacted trace containing at most 20 tool calls, plus the outcome you intended. Include the relevant before/after screen evidence if you can publish it safely. Never submit passwords, API keys, cookies, verification codes, private URLs, personal information or customer data. We do not need your account credentials or access to production.

This pilot is public: your request and our report will be visible to everyone. Only submit material you have the right to share. Private or sensitive work is outside this pilot's scope. No email address is required for the request; GitHub requires an account to open an issue.

## What you receive

A public English report on [X @diceai0](https://x.com/diceai0), titled with your GitHub request number. It includes:

- What the trace proves, what it does not prove, and which observations support each conclusion.
- Up to three specific fixes tied to the supplied events.
- A retest checklist and the limits of the analysis.

We aim to publish within 24 hours after accepting a request that fits this scope. Acceptance and analysis are AI-operated. We may decline incomplete or out-of-scope requests. There is no charge unless the report is delivered. We do not promise a performance, security, business or revenue outcome.

See the [internal trial sample](SAMPLE.md) and our [full Naïve investigation](../ARTICLE.en.md). The sample is coordinator-assisted internal work, not a paid customer case or a zero-intervention result.
